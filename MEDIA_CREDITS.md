# Media credits

All photographs in `public/media/photos/` come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences (or the public domain).
They were found by `scripts/media/fetch-candidates.mjs` (licence filter: CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain; files tagged as AI-generated excluded), then chosen by eye.
Files were only resized and converted to WebP — no retouching, no AI processing. CC BY-SA images remain under CC BY-SA.

On the site they illustrate **places**, never the fictional stories: every caption says so, and `/credits` lists them.
